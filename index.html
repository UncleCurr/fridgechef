How hybrid inference works here

On-device · quick ideas

Simple requests are handled on your device with Gemini Nano — fast, private, and free — and returned as structured recipe data.

Cloud · full recipes

When the request is complex — many ingredients, dietary limits, longer recipes — it falls back to Gemini in the cloud.

Structured output

Recipes come back as structured JSON (title, time, difficulty, ingredients, steps), so the app can render them as clean, usable recipe cards.

Built with Firebase AI Logic — Gemini Nano on-device + Gemini in the cloud. Free to use. Happy cooking.
